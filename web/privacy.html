Privacy

Last updated 16 August 2026

The short version

Wren has no servers, no accounts and no analytics. It collects nothing about you, because there is nowhere for it to send anything. Your screenshots are read on your phone and are never uploaded.

Who this is

Wren is made by Spencer Fields, a sole trader established in London, United Kingdom. For anything in this policy, write to Apps [at] spencerfields [dot] com.

What Wren collects

Nothing. There is no account to create, no sign-in, no profile, no analytics or crash-reporting service, and no server operated by Wren for your data to reach. Everything Wren remembers lives on your device and goes away when you delete the app.

Your screenshots

You choose which images Wren may read, through Apple's own photo picker. Wren has no standing access to your photo library — only to the images you hand it.

Text recognition runs entirely on your device, using Apple's Vision framework. The images themselves are never uploaded, transmitted or stored by Wren. Once a name has been read from an image, Wren has no further use for it and does not keep a copy.

What leaves your device, and where it goes

Only text, and only to Apple.

WhatGoes toWhy
The name of a place, and the city you confirmed Apple Maps To find the place. This is Apple's ordinary Maps search, made from your device in the same way the Maps app makes it.
The city or region you confirm Apple Maps To turn a name like "London" into a location, so the search looks in the right place.
The places you choose to publish Apple Maps Wren opens a link that creates the guide. The places travel inside that link.
Purchase requests Apple Handled entirely by the App Store. Wren never sees your payment details, name or address.

Apple's handling of these is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not this one. Wren sends nothing to anybody else — no third-party service, no advertiser, no analytics provider.

Instagram and other apps

Wren does not connect to Instagram, TikTok or any other service. It has no access to your accounts on them and does not read, scrape or download anything from them. It only reads screenshots you have taken and chosen to share with it.

What is stored on your device

  • Whether you have bought the one-time unlock, so you are not asked twice.
  • The list of places you are working on, until you publish or clear it.

Both are ordinary app storage on your phone, covered by your device's own encryption. Deleting Wren deletes them.

Children

Wren is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR you have rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection and portability over personal data held about you. Wren holds none, so in practice there is nothing to access, correct or erase — but if you believe otherwise, write to the address above and it will be dealt with within one month.

If you are in the United Kingdom, you may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and includes no tracking of any kind. The hosting provider keeps ordinary server access logs, which is normal for any website and is not something Wren reads or uses.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Material changes will be noted on this page rather than made quietly.
