Little Bird

A little bird told me.

Someone sends you a reel of the five places you have to eat at in Rome. You watch it, you mean to remember, and it is gone by Thursday.

Little Bird catches them. Screenshot the places as they appear, and it reads the names off the screen, works out which restaurants they actually are, and drops the lot into a guide in Apple Maps — where you will be standing when you need them.

  1. 01 Screenshot what you see One tap per place, straight from the reel. Nothing is sent to Instagram and nothing is scraped.
  2. 02 It reads the names Text recognition runs on your phone, so the screenshots stay there.
  3. 03 Straight into Apple Maps Not a copy of your places in another app — an actual guide, in the Maps you already use.
Where it is up to

Being built. It is not on the App Store yet, and there is nothing to download.

The awkward part — getting places into Apple Maps in bulk, which Apple provides no way to do — is solved and working on a real device. What remains is the app around it.
