Where to get Wren.

iPhone

Wren is on the App Store

Get Wren for iPhone — free to download, and it needs iOS 18 or later.

Free covers guides of up to three places. A single one-time purchase removes that limit and lets Wren add to a guide you already have. It is not a subscription: nothing renews and nothing expires. The terms set that out.

Android

Being built, for the Play Store

Wren for Android will arrive through the Play Store.

Android has no equivalent of an Apple Maps guide, so the app works the way Android works: Wren reads the same screenshots, files and links, checks every place with you, and then hands the list to whichever map app you already use.
